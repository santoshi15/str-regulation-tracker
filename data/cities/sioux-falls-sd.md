# Sioux Falls, SD: Short Term Rental Rules

Last verified: 2026-10-25
Summary for tracker research only. This file is general information and is not legal advice. Confirm a specific address with the city before buying or listing.

## Permit or license required and type
- City Residential Rental Permit is required for long term and short term residential rental units, effective January 1, 2024. A permit is required per rental street address and must be issued before advertisement, soliciting, or occupancy.
- Short term rental definition used by the city: a home, cabin, or similar building rented, leased, or furnished in its entirety to the public on a daily or weekly basis for more than 14 days in a calendar year, where the unit is not occupied by an owner or manager during the rental. Bed and breakfast establishments under ordinance 160.005 are excluded from that definition.
- Applicants for a short term rental unit must also show proof of a state sales tax license and proof of a state health department lodging license, plus any other state license required by law.

## Registration steps and fees
- Steps listed by the city: provide owner name, email, mailing address, and phone; provide a local contact within 50 miles if the owner lives more than 50 miles from city limits; provide street address and number and type of units; provide manager license information if a licensed broker, broker associate, property manager, or residential rental agent manages the unit; complete the required training or show the manager license exemption; submit proof of state sales tax license and state health department lodging license for a short term rental.
- Training: a minimum of two hours of city approved course curriculum is required before permit approval. The training exemption applies when the property will be managed by a person licensed with the South Dakota Real Estate Commission as listed on the city training page.
- Fee: $50 permit fee per address after July 1, 2024. The city page says the fee is waived for new construction property that receives a permit prior to tenant occupancy, once unless ownership or contacts change, and that transfer notice is due in writing within 30 days.

## Lodging and transient tax rate and who collects
- South Dakota Department of Revenue municipal tax guidance lists, for lodging accommodations for periods of less than 28 consecutive days: Tourism Tax 1.5% and Sioux Falls Lodging 1% as other tax types, plus state and municipal sales tax that applies by jurisdiction. The city permit page separately requires proof of a state sales tax license for a short term rental applicant, and it does not publish the combined rate.

## Enforcement and penalties summary
- Permits can be revoked when the city issues a fourth health, nuisance, or other violation after the permit holder has been subject to three health, nuisance, or other code violations resulting in citations and fines payable to the city within the previous three calendar years, excluding snow and tree tickets. The city page states the owner gets a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue, appeals go through an independent hearing examiner after written notice within 15 days with a $50 appeal processing fee, and a renewal permit after revocation requires correction of all violations, city inspection approval, and payment of all citations.
- Dollar fine for operating without a permit: UNVERIFIED in the primary source reviewed. I checked the city Residential Rental Permit page on 2026-10-25 and found revocation language and no general operating without a permit fine amount on that page.
