## Permit or license required and type
- A Vacation Rental Registration Certificate is required. Palm Springs Municipal Code Section 5.25.040(a) prohibits operation of a vacation rental or a homeshare without a Vacation Rental Registration Certificate, as quoted in city administrative citation documents.
- A Transient Occupancy Tax registration and permit are also required. The city close out form states the form closes a vacation rental or homeshare property TOT permit and Registration Certificate, and it states a new owner must apply for a new certificate with a new application.
- A vacation rental is defined in Chapter 5.25 as a single family dwelling, or any portion of one, used for occupancy for 28 consecutive days or less while the owner is not present, excluding month to month tenancy to the same renter, timeshare, and condominium hotel as defined in the code.

## Permit cap and waitlist status
- Neighborhood cap: 20 percent. Chapter 5.25 defines the VR Neighborhood Percentage Cap at 20 percent, calculated as homes with vacation rental registration certificates, excluding junior certificates, divided by total residential dwelling units in the neighborhood. The cap may be adjusted by council by ordinance or resolution.
- Waitlist: the code structure provides for waiting applications to be served when a neighborhood falls below the cap. ## Registration steps and fees
- Steps confirmed from city documents: apply for a Registration Certificate before operating or advertising, obtain a TOT permit, complete the close out process on sale and have the new owner apply anew, and file TOT returns monthly. The city close out page states typical lead time for processing an application is 30 days, application fees are not refundable even if a sale does not go through, and advertising or operating without a Registration Certificate is prohibited.

## Lodging and transient tax rate and who collects
- Transient Occupancy Tax: 11.5 percent on short term stays of 28 consecutive days or less, as stated in a city administrative document summarizing the application terms. A TOT return must be filed monthly even if no tax is due, and returns must be received by the city TOT desk by 6 p.m. on the last working day of the month following the month at issue, per that document.
- Tourism Business Improvement District assessment: 1 percent of vacation rental gross short term rental revenue for stays of less than 28 days, paid at the same time as monthly TOT payments, per the city finance page. That page states the Greater Palm Springs TBID renewal in 2020 levied 1 percent on vacation rentals, effective July 1, 2021 for vacation rental and homeshare lodgings, and that a TOT and TBID return must be provided every month whether the property was rented or not.
- Collection: the operator files and pays TOT and TBID to the city monthly, as described in the city sources above. Platform collection was not verified from a primary source in this session.
- Sources: City of Palm Springs, Tourism Business Improvement District, https://www.palmspringsca.gov/government/departments/finance-treasury/tourism-business-improvement-district/-fsiteid-1 , verified 2026-10-25; City of Palm Springs administrative document stating TOT of 11.5 percent, https://www.palmspringsca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/81572/637807004294100000 , verified 2026-10-25

## Enforcement and penalties summary
- Operating without a Registration Certificate: city citation documents state the operator is responsible for back registration certificate fees and penalties and back TOT, penalties, and interest under Chapter 3.24, and they cite Section 5.25.090(c) for fines and penalties for operation without a certificate.
- One city citation document states each future violation from continued operation as an unpermitted vacation rental will result in an administrative fine of not less than $10,000, plus possible civil or criminal remedies, and it describes permanent ineligibility as a penalty that the city stayed under a corrective action plan in that case. Treat the $10,000 and ineligibility language as case document language, and confirm the current code penalty schedule in Section 5.25.090 before relying on it for a specific property.
- Sources: City of Palm Springs administrative citation document, https://www.palmspringsca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/53428/636357325197570000 , verified 2026-10-25; City of Palm Springs administrative citation document, https://www.palmspringsca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/85309/638204571034270000 , verified 2026-10-25

## Recent or pending changes
- Rules in flux: yes at the code level. Chapter 5.25 publisher text shows a long amendment history including Ordinance 2075 in 2022 and Ordinance 2133 dated July 8, 2026 in the code excerpt retrieved.
- Co owned housing: City Council adopted Ordinance 2100 on July 9, 2024 establishing a program for Co Owned Housing Units with a limit of 30 total units and per neighborhood limits after vacation rental neighborhood caps, per the city co owned housing page. That program is adjacent to vacation rentals and affects buyers considering fractional ownership structures.
- A Vacation Rental and Homeshare Workgroup page states the workgroup provides input on density, ancillary use, and housing supply and makes recommendations for future policy.
