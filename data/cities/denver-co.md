# Denver, CO: Short Term Rental Rules

Last verified: 2026-10-25
Jurisdiction: City and County of Denver.
Summary for tracker research only. This file is general information and is not legal advice.

## Permit or license required and type
- A short term rental business license is required. The city laws page states all short term rentals must have a license, any short term rental must be the host primary residence, and all advertisements must display the host business license number.
- A short term rental is a residential dwelling unit available for rent for 1 to 29 days.
- A Lodger Tax license from the Department of Finance is also required, per the city Tax Guide Topic 97, in addition to the business license from the Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection.
- Licenses expire annually and must be renewed online before expiration.

## Registration steps and fees
- Application documents for all applicants: a copy of a valid Colorado driver license or Colorado identification card, plus at least two of the following showing full name and address: proof of valid motor vehicle registration, proof of voter registration, federal or state tax returns for the most current tax year, financial documentation such as bank or credit card statements, or a recent utility bill showing service and mailing address as a downloadable PDF.
- Applicants who do not own the property must also provide a signed possession of property certificate.
- Specialist review can take up to 90 days from a complete application, initial reviews can take up to 30 days, and a request for added information must be fulfilled within 14 days or the application moves to inactive, per the city pages.
- License fee amount for the STR license: UNVERIFIED in the city pages retrieved in this session. I opened the new application page and the renewal page on 2026-10-25 and those pages describe documents, display duties, renewal, insurance, and tax duties, with no fee amount stated in the text retrieved. I excluded secondary fee figures.
- Renewal timing: licenses expire annually, renewal must be filed online before expiration, a 90 day grace period applies with a 20 percent penalty fee in the first 30 days and a 50 percent penalty fee on days 31 to 90, and after 90 days a new license is required, per the renewal page summary in search.

## Owner occupancy rule
- Primary residence required. The city defines primary residence as the place where a person habitation is fixed for the term of the license and is the person usual place of return. A person can have only one primary residence.
- The Denver Revised Municipal Code criteria the department may consider include: other locations claimed for domestic, legal, billing, voting, or licensing purposes; how often the applicant returns to the unit or resides elsewhere; address differences on legal documents or tax assessment records; business, employment, income, leasehold, and motor vehicle registration indicators; how much time the unit has been or will be rented in a calendar year; and active military deployment status.
- License holders must maintain the unit as their primary residence and may rent under only one reservation or booking at a time.
- Sources: City and County of Denver, Short term rental frequently asked questions, https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Business-Licensing/Business-licenses/Short-term-rentals/Short-term-rental-FAQ?lang_update=638598197018372468 , verified 2026-10-25; City and County of Denver, New short term rental application, https://denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Business-Licensing/Business-licenses/Short-term-rentals/New , verified 2026-10-25

## Lodging and transient tax rate and who collects
- Denver Lodger Tax: 10.75 percent of the entire amount charged for lodging, for stays where the guest is not a permanent resident and has not entered a written agreement for at least 30 consecutive days and paid for at least 30 days, per the city Business Tax FAQ.
- Filing frequency is based on liability: monthly when average monthly liability for the prior six months exceeds $1,100, quarterly when it averages $1,100 or less, and annual when it averages $50 or less, with due dates on the 20th day as described on that FAQ page.
- Operators must also handle Occupational Privilege Tax accounts as applicable. The city STR taxation sheet lists Lodger Tax with a license fee, Occupational Privilege Tax, and sales tax where applicable, and it states the Lodger Tax license is issued on a bi annual basis through the eBiz Tax portal.
- Host duties: check Lodger Tax and Occupational Privilege Tax accounts and file on time even when a platform collects tax, and renew the tax account every odd year, per the application pages.
- Sources: City and County of Denver, Business Tax FAQs, https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Department-of-Finance/Our-Divisions/Treasury/Business-Tax-Information/Business-Tax-FAQ?lang_update=638575807439653957 , verified 2026-10-25; City and County of Denver, Short Term Rental Taxation Information, https://www.denvergov.org/files/assets/public/v/1/finance/documents/treasury/shorttermrentaltaxationinformation26.pdf , verified 2026-10-25; City and County of Denver, New short term rental application, https://denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Business-Licensing/Business-licenses/Short-term-rentals/New , verified 2026-10-25

## Enforcement and penalties summary
- The city laws page states hosts who do not follow the rules could be fined or could lose their license, and the main page states it is unlawful to operate with an expired license unless a complete renewal application is pending and unlawful to advertise without the license number displayed.
- Insurance duty that ties to enforcement: at least $1 million in liability insurance covering STR use, or listing only through platforms that provide that coverage in their service agreement.
