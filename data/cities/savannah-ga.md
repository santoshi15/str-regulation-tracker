# Savannah, GA: Short Term Vacation Rental Rules

Last verified: 2026-10-25
Summary for tracker research only. This file is general information and is not legal advice.

## Permit or license required and type
- A Short Term Vacation Rental certificate, called an STVR certificate, is required. The city states STVR managers must complete an application process and secure an STVR certificate to operate legally within the city.
- Definition: an accommodation for transient guests where, for compensation, a residential dwelling unit is provided for lodging for a period not to exceed 30 consecutive days. The city zoning page clarifies an STVR is the rental of an entire dwelling unit for 30 days or less. Rental of a room inside a dwelling is regulated separately as a bed and breakfast homestay.
- Location limit: STVRs are permitted within the STVR Overlay District, covering the Downtown, Victorian, and Streetcar local historic districts, and outside the overlay only in certain Business and Agriculture zoning classifications listed by the city as B-C, B-N, B-L, and A-1.
- A Business Tax Certificate and a Business Location Approval are the general business steps for businesses in Savannah, per the city business pages. STVR specific tax filing is covered in the tax section.
- Sources: City of Savannah, STVR Short Term Vacation Rentals, https://www.savannahga.gov/1476/Short-Term-Vacation-Rentals , verified 2026-10-25; City of Savannah, Short Term Vacation Rental Regulations, https://www.savannahga.gov/2327/STVR-Regulations , verified 2026-10-25

## Permit cap and waitlist status
- Per ward cap: 20 percent of residential parcels within each ward for new STVRs in residential areas in the Downtown and Victorian districts. Owner occupied parcels are exempt from this cap. Applications filed before September 28, 2017 are grandfathered and can be renewed.
- Capped wards status on the city regulations page retrieved: every ward listed for the Historic District and every ward listed for the Victorian District is marked as having met the 20 percent cap. The Historic District list includes Anson, Bartow, Berrien, Brown, Calhoun, Charlton, Chatham, Columbia, Crawford, Currietown, Davis, Forsyth, Pulaski, Percival, Gaston, Green, Jasper, Jackson, Lafayette, Mercer, Monterey, ONeil, Stephens, Troup, Trustees Garden, Walton, Warren, Washington, and Wesley wards. The Victorian District list includes Barry, Cumming, Cuthbert, Elliot, Falligant, Gallie, Gue, Lee, Lester, Lloyd, Reppard, Waring, White, and Wylly wards.
- Waitlist: yes. A property owner seeking a non owner occupied STVR certificate in a capped ward should submit the STVR Waiting List Form. The city will contact the applicant when an opening occurs, and the waitlist is first come, first served.
- Transfers: STVR certificates are non transferable under Section 8-11012. A title transfer for a parcel with a pre September 28, 2017 certificate can support a new application by the transferee within six months without denial on cap grounds, per the city page.
- Source: City of Savannah, Short Term Vacation Rental Regulations, https://www.savannahga.gov/2327/STVR-Regulations , verified 2026-10-25

## Registration steps and fees
- New STVR application fee: $400, non refundable.
- Annual renewal application fee: $250, non refundable.
- Applications go through the STVR Registration Portal, per the city page, with payment by ACH debit or credit or debit card at checkout plus a vendor processing fee as described in the city Q and A document.
- Renewal timing from the city Q and A: permits expire one year from issuance, annual renewals are due 30 days before expiration, renewals are unavailable after a permit expires, and a closed permit receives no exceptions or waivers.
- Application duties summarized on the regulations page: notify all of record adjacent property owners before issuance of an initial certificate with the address, parking location, maximum occupancy, a copy of the exemplar rental agreement, owner name, and rental agent contact; provide an exemplar rental agreement that must be posted in the property; provide proof of insurance showing the premise is used as an STVR for new and renewing applications; publish the certificate number and the exemplar agreement in every advertisement and listing; and notify the city within five business days when the rental agent changes.
- Zoning Confirmation Letter request fee: $50, for zoning eligibility and parking questions.
- Sources: City of Savannah, STVR Short Term Vacation Rentals, https://www.savannahga.gov/1476/Short-Term-Vacation-Rentals , verified 2026-10-25; City of Savannah, Short Term Vacation Rental Regulations, https://www.savannahga.gov/2327/STVR-Regulations , verified 2026-10-25; City of Savannah, STVR Q and A, https://savannahga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/30786/Updated-STVR-QA-Announcement?bidId= , verified 2026-10-25

## Owner occupancy rule
- Owner occupied parcels are exempt from the 20 percent per ward cap. The regulations page defines owner occupied property as property with one or more dwelling units where the principal dwelling unit is occupied by the owner as the owner primary and usual place of residence, with units sharing the Property Identification Number from the Chatham County Board of Assessors.
- Streetcar District rule: all STVRs on parcels zoned TN-2 must have two or more dwelling units, and one unit must be owner occupied, per Section 7.5.4 as summarized by the city.
- Source: City of Savannah, Short Term Vacation Rental Regulations, https://www.savannahga.gov/2327/STVR-Regulations , verified 2026-10-25

## Minimum stay rule
- Definition threshold: 30 consecutive days or less is an STVR, with hotel motel tax applying to the first 30 days of a continuous stay as described in the tax section.
- Minimum night stay per booking: UNVERIFIED. I checked the city STVR pages on 2026-10-25 and found the 30 day definition with no minimum stay rule stated.

## Occupancy limits
- Dwelling units with no more than two bedrooms: occupants shall not exceed four adults.
- Dwelling units with three or more bedrooms: occupants shall not exceed two adults per bedroom.
- Bedrooms are subject to verification of building code compliance by the Zoning Administrator. Occupants must acknowledge maximum occupancy and, if available, on site parking location.
- Source: City of Savannah, Short Term Vacation Rental Regulations, https://www.savannahga.gov/2327/STVR-Regulations , verified 2026-10-25

## Lodging and transient tax rate and who collects
- City Hotel Motel Excise Tax: 8 percent of taxable charges to the public for lodging accommodations within the City of Savannah, per the city ordinance amendment text in the city agenda system stating the rate shall be 8 percent and revenues shall be used under OCGA 48-13-51(b). The city Excise Tax Compliance Unit page lists Hotel Motel Tax Monthly Return forms at a 6 percent rate and at an 8 percent rate, which reflects the rate change history, and it states returns and payments are due on the 20th of the month following collection.
- History that explains the two forms: a 2023 city resolution in the agenda system states the General Assembly and Governor passed legislation in the 2023 session enabling the city to adjust the hotel motel tax from 6 percent to 8 percent, and the city desired to adopt an 8 percent rate.
- Collection: each person or firm collecting the tax must transmit a return by the 20th day of each month showing total charges for the preceding month and remit the tax to the Revenue Department, per the ordinance text. Georgia sales tax also applies to lodging and is handled through the state, and a specific combined total is UNVERIFIED from a Savannah primary source in this session.
- Status caution: because the city site lists both 6 percent and 8 percent return forms, confirm the rate that applies to a specific STVR account with the Revenue Department before filing. The ordinance text retrieved states 8 percent.
- Sources: City of Savannah, ordinance amendment Article E Hotel Motel Tax, https://agenda.savannahga.gov/content/files/article-e-hotel-motel-tax-amendment-052423.pdf , verified 2026-10-25; City of Savannah, Excise Tax Compliance Unit, https://savannahga.gov/4226/Excise-Tax-Compliance-Unit , verified 2026-10-25; City of Savannah, May 25, 2023 agenda item on the hotel motel tax rate, https://agenda.savannahga.gov/publishing/may-25-2023-city-council-regular-meeting/1394_9406.html , verified 2026-10-25

## Enforcement and penalties summary
- STVR certificates are non transferable, renewals for grandfathered certificates receive cap protection as described above, and a permit closed after expiration receives no exceptions or waivers per the city Q and A.
- Late hotel motel tax payment penalty in the return form: 5 percent of the net tax or $5, whichever is greater, when postmarked after the 20th and the failure is no more than 30 days, plus an added 5 percent or $5 per added 30 day period, capped at 25 percent or $25 in aggregate, whichever is greater, as printed on the city return form.
