# Asheville, NC: Homestay and Short Term Rental Rules

Last verified: 2026-10-25
Jurisdiction: City of Asheville, with Buncombe County tax and county zoning applying outside city limits.
Summary for tracker research only. This file is general information and is not legal advice.
Scope warning: city rules and unincorporated Buncombe County rules differ in a material way. Always confirm whether an address sits inside Asheville city limits.

## Permit or license required and type
- City Homestay Permit: required to rent part of a home while living there. The city compliance page calls this lodging a homestay and lists the permit conditions in the owner occupancy and room limit sections.
- Whole home rentals inside the city: a rental of an entire house or apartment for less than a month is a Short Term Vacation Rental, called STVR, and STVRs are only allowed in the Resort Zoning District, per the city compliance page.
- The city news pages add that STRs can only be permitted in commercially zoned areas and in certain non residential areas that allow lodging, and that residential district whole home STRs are prohibited. The compliance page Resort Zoning District statement is the current page I relied on for the tracker entry, and a buyer should confirm zoning for a specific parcel with Development Services.

## Owner occupancy rule
- Homestay: the host must live in the house or apartment full time. The city program page states a permanent resident must reside at the property and be present in the home during the homestay.
- Room limit tied to that rule: only one or two bedrooms may be rented.
- Whole home STVR in the city is limited to the Resort Zoning District on the compliance page, so a whole home rental in a residential district fails the zoning test regardless of owner occupancy.
- Sources: City of Asheville, Homestays and Short Term Rental Violations, https://www.ashevillenc.gov/department/development-services/compliance-division/homestays-and-short-term-rental-violations/?lang=es , verified 2026-10-25; City of Asheville, Homestay and short term rental regulation program progressing, https://www.ashevillenc.gov/news/ashevilles-homestay-and-short-term-rental-regulation-program-progressing/ , verified 2026-10-25

## Enforcement and penalties summary
- Complaints go through the Asheville App to the Compliance Division. Staff works Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., and staff may investigate after hours.
- Historic enforcement summary on the city update page: once a resident with an illegal homestay or STR received notice, they had 30 days to become compliant before a fine was issued, and the city described active enforcement with a dedicated homestay and STR enforcement employee. That page dates to the 2015 and 2016 program updates, so treat the 30 day cure language as program history and confirm current penalty amounts with the Compliance Division.

## Recent or pending changes
- Program history that still shapes the current rules: in November 2015 City Council adopted homestay updates that clarified definitions, reduced rentable bedrooms from three to two, and changed the home percentage rule, and in May 2016 updates increased the zoning districts where homestays may be allowed and removed the 500 foot separation requirement, per city news pages.
- Pending change status in 2026: UNVERIFIED. I checked the city compliance page on 2026-10-25 and found the current four homestay rules and the Resort District limit for whole home rentals, with no pending amendment notice on that page.
